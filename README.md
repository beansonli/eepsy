## ℹ️ About
**Eepsy** is a lightweight, open-source Android utility designed to help you disconnect. If you use Bluetooth headphones to fall asleep to music or podcasts, Eepsy manages your connection so you don't wake up to battery drain or accidental playback.

<a name="features"></a>
## ✨ Features
* **Custom Presets:** Create up to 20 custom timer durations.
* **Drag-and-Drop:** Easily reorder your timers to keep your favorites at the top.
* **Smart UI:** Adapts to your system’s Light or Dark mode.
* **Zero Bloat:** No ads, no tracking, no unnecessary permissions.
> Also keeps your timer running reliably in the background :D

<a name="installation"></a>
## Quick Download & Install
Since eepsy is open-source and distributed directly through GitHub, you won't find it on the Google Play Store. Installing it is safe and straightforward!

### Steps to install:
1. **Visit the [Releases Page](../../releases).**
2. Click on the **Latest** release at the top.
3. Look for the **Assets** section and download the `.apk` file.
4. **Open the file** from your "Downloads" folder.
5. **Security Prompt:** If Android warns you about installing from "Unknown Sources," tap **Settings** on the prompt, toggle **"Allow from this source,"** and return to the installation.
6. Tap **Install** and enjoy!

*Note: Your device is secure. This is standard behavior for any app downloaded outside of the official Google Play Store.*

<a name="troubleshooting"></a>
## 📝 Troubleshooting
* **App won't install?** Ensure you have uninstalled any previous version of the app before installing a new one (sometimes signature conflicts can occur).
* **Bluetooth connection issues?** Make sure the app has the necessary Bluetooth permissions in your Android System Settings.
